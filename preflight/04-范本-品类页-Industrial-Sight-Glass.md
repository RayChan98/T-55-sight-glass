## What Is an Industrial Sight Glass?

An industrial sight glass (also called a sight glass window, sight port, or inspection window) is a transparent viewing window installed in a pressure vessel, tank, or pipeline. It lets operators **visually inspect process media** — fluid level, flow, color, clarity, mixing, and contamination — without opening the system.

Sight glasses are critical in chemical processing, pharmaceutical production, food & beverage, brewing, oil & gas, and steam systems. They are often used with sight glass lights to illuminate tank and pipe interiors.

> 💡 Glass is often the weakest link in any process system. That's why sight glass selection — glass type, metal housing, and pressure rating — is a safety-critical engineering decision.

## How to Select an Industrial Sight Glass: 6 Key Questions

### 1. What Pressure Do I Need?
The glass material, unsupported diameter, and glass thickness determine the pressure capability of a sight glass. Pressure ratings are listed on each product datasheet. Higher-pressure applications (steam, compressed gas) require thicker, fused glass windows.

### 2. What Temperature Do I Need?
| Glass Type | Temperature Range | Best For |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Soda lime glass | Below 300°F (150°C) | General purpose, low-cost applications |
| **Borosilicate glass** | Up to 600°F (316°C) | High-temperature processes, thermal shock resistance |
| Quartz / sapphire glass | Above 600°F | Extreme high-temperature steam applications |
| Mica shields | — | Extreme conditions, protective layer |

Borosilicate glass resists thermal shock better than soda lime — essential when hot equipment is washed down with cool water.

### 3. What Metal Housing Is Best?
Stainless steel offers corrosion resistance and is standard in pharmaceutical applications. **316 stainless steel** is the most common grade; duplex stainless steel offers even higher corrosion resistance for aggressive chemical media. Carbon steel suits general industrial use; Hastelloy is available for severe chemical service.

### 4. What Applications Suit Which Glass?
Corrosive and abrasive process media can cause glass to become hazy. Borosilicate glass performs better than soda lime in these conditions and is the recommended default for chemical and pharmaceutical processes.

### 5. What Gasket Do I Need?
Gasket material must be compatible with your process media and temperature. Available options include PTFE, EPDM, Viton™, and silicone — specify your media for a recommendation.

### 6. Fused vs. Standard Glass Windows
Fused sight glass windows (glass fused to a metal ring) hold the glass in concentric compression, making it resistant to bending stresses that cause cracks or failure. **Fused windows are preferred for high-pressure ratings, impact resistance, thermal shock resistance, and high safety margins** — the same construction used in the industry's leading METAGLAS®-style designs.
